A Little Promise For Sadaa

Made with love, honesty and a heart that wants to do better.

Dear Sadaa, 🌷

I know there are moments from the past that still hurt you. I know saying sorry cannot erase those memories. And I don't expect you to suddenly forget everything.

But I want you to know something... Your feelings matter to me, even when I don't understand them immediately.

Things I Never Want You To Forget 💗

You are not difficult to love.
Your feelings are not a burden.
Your tears are not meaningless.
Your past pain deserves to be heard.
And you deserve honesty, respect and peace.

My Little Promises 🤍

♡ I will choose honesty over hiding things.

♡ I will listen instead of getting defensive.

♡ I will respect your feelings, even during arguments.

♡ I will not expect instant forgiveness.

♡ I will show my change through consistent actions.

A Little Memory 🌸

Out of all the little things in this world, your smile is something I never want to take for granted. Your love is not something I want to win for seven days. I want to become someone who treats it carefully every day.

Open Your Little Surprise 🎁

If I could give you one thing, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes. You would understand how special you are, how beautiful your heart is, and how much you deserve to be loved correctly. I'm sorry for the times I made you feel otherwise. ❤️

One Last Thing 🥺

I cannot change yesterday. I cannot demand your trust. I cannot force your heart to heal. But I can take responsibility for my actions, be patient with your healing, and keep showing you that my words mean something. No pressure. No deadlines. Just genuine effort.

With love, always ❤️

— Talha
